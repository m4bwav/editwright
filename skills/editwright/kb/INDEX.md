- [levels.md](levels.md): read when deciding what a pass may touch, what it delivers and the order of passes.
- [deliverables.md](deliverables.md): read when writing an editorial letter, margin notes, author queries, a handover, a style sheet, story bible or scene sheet.
- [fiction.md](fiction.md): read when diagnosing a story or novel (POV, scenes, stakes, pacing, dialogue, arc, openings, endings).
- [story-checks.md](story-checks.md): read during a fiction developmental or line pass for named checks with codes, and what a cut must keep.
- [conflict-scenes.md](conflict-scenes.md): read when a quarrel or confrontation reads flat or repetitive (tag each line by stress behaviour).
- [short-story.md](short-story.md): read when the piece is flash to novella or headed to a magazine or contest.
- [line-copy-proof.md](line-copy-proof.md): read when running a line, copy or proof pass, weighing a repeated fix, or reading ew.py stats.
- [long-manuscripts.md](long-manuscripts.md): read when the manuscript runs 80k to 150k words.
- [syntax.md](syntax.md): read during a line or copy pass in any genre for agreement, paired markers, garden paths, misattached modifiers, centre-embedding, heavy last and known before new.
- [nonfiction.md](nonfiction.md): read when checking an argument or facts, or choosing a style guide.
- [clarity.md](clarity.md): read during a nonfiction line pass for zombie nouns, light verbs, metaconcepts, scare quotes, hedges, abbreviations and role names.
- [structure.md](structure.md): read during a structural pass on reports, memos, briefs and papers (containers, maps, roadsigns, default orders), and for legal and business documents.
- [argument.md](argument.md): read during a developmental pass on persuasive nonfiction (how many reasons, which first, objections, concessions, the close), for paragraph links, the first-sentence skim and bracketed cuts.
- [scripts.md](scripts.md): read when editing a screenplay or stage play, or writing coverage.
- [ai-text-rules.md](ai-text-rules.md): read before any replacement, and when an author asks about watermarks, detectors or disclosure.
- [delivery.md](delivery.md): read when choosing output formats (markdown, CriticMarkup, .docx, Google Docs).

Related: [levels.md](levels.md), [ai-text-rules.md](ai-text-rules.md)
